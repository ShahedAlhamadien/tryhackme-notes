## Overview
This room introduces the perks of working in cyber security and the different roles available in the field. At the end, an interactive quiz recommends a role that fits your personality and working style.

## Key Terms
- **Pentester (Penetration Tester)** = someone who thinks like an attacker and tests systems and applications step by step to find weaknesses before real hackers do.
- **Learning Path** = a structured series of rooms that takes you from beginner level to a specific career role.
